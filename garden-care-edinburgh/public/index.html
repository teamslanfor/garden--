Who we are

Local gardeners who treat your garden like their own.

Garden Care is an Edinburgh gardening business based in Polwarth. We do grass cutting, hedge trimming and full garden clearances for homes across the city.

Whether your lawn needs a regular trim, your hedges have got away from you, or the whole garden needs clearing and starting again, we turn up, do the job properly and leave everything tidy. We’re open seven days a week from 8am to 8pm, so it’s easy to fit us in around your week.
