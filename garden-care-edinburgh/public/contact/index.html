FAQs

Quick answers.

How do I get a price?

Call 07414 080999, send a WhatsApp or use the quote form. Tell us what you need and roughly how big the garden is. Photos help us quote accurately.

When are you open?

Every day, Monday to Sunday, from 8am to 8pm.

Do you take away the clippings and waste?

We always clear up clippings and leave the garden tidy. Let us know about larger amounts of waste from clearances when you ask for a quote, and we’ll confirm how it will be handled.

Do I need to be home?

Not usually, as long as we can get into the garden. We’ll agree access when booking.

Which areas do you cover?

We’re based in Polwarth (EH11) and work across Edinburgh. Get in touch with your postcode and we’ll confirm.
