About Garden Care

Local gardeners. Honest work. Tidy results.

We’re a hands-on Edinburgh gardening business that keeps the city’s gardens neat, healthy and enjoyable, seven days a week.

Our story

Rooted in Polwarth, working across Edinburgh.

Garden Care is based at Polwarth Gardens in Edinburgh. We focus on the jobs that make the biggest difference to a garden: grass cutting, hedge trimming and full garden clearances.

Lots of people love having a garden but don’t have the time or energy to keep on top of it. That’s where we come in. Whether it’s a small city plot or a larger family garden, a one-off job or regular upkeep, we bring the tools and do the work. When we leave, your garden is tidy and ready to enjoy.

We’re open from 8am to 8pm every day of the week, so booking a visit that fits your schedule is easy.
