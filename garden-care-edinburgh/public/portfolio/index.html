Typical jobs

How we handle the jobs we’re asked for most.

Example scenarios showing our approach. Each garden is quoted individually.

Full clearanceOvergrown garden

Neglected garden, fresh start

The challenge
A garden left for a few seasons, with long grass, weeds and overgrown shrubs.
Our approach
Cut back, dig out unwanted growth, clear beds and remove the debris.
The aim
A clean, usable garden that can be enjoyed or replanted.
Hedge trimmingBoundary hedge

Overgrown hedge, back in shape

The challenge
A boundary hedge blocking light and spilling onto the pavement.
Our approach
Cut back and shape the top and sides, then collect all clippings.
The aim
A neat, healthy hedge and a tidy frontage.
Grass cuttingRegular visits

A lawn that stays neat

The challenge
No time to keep up with the lawn through the growing season.
Our approach
Regular cuts with edging, on a schedule that suits.
The aim
A consistently tidy lawn without lifting a finger.
